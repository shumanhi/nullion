# Nullion v0.11.2

Nullion v0.11.2 improves browser reliability, task progress, and everyday chat delivery.

- Reconnect to the selected browser while preserving your tabs and useful page state.
- Get readable website screenshots, with full-page coverage when requested.
- See clearer progress and approval requests, and receive final replies even when planner cards are hidden.
- Recover attachments and quoted messages without confusing them with older files or conversations.
- Recall relevant saved conversations and keep file delivery tied to your current request.
- Read cleaner email previews with correct text encoding and less repeated confirmation text.
- Create more readable PDFs with cleaner page breaks, less wasted space, and complete text and images.
- Review accurate deletion targets, including commands that discard error output, and receive clearer confirmation of what changed.
- Recover interrupted requests and live trackers while retaining completed work.
